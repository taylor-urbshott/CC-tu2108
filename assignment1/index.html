Procession

by Spatial Pixel

Procession is a natural language IDE that uses a camera, microphone, projector, and AI agent to build digital interactions in physical space. This was created with the goal of engaging users in a spatial technology that does not require direct interaction with a screen. Creation is key, with the output of the experience being an immersive projected experience of the user's own making.

What I find interesting about this work is the leverage of a person's body and occupied space while engaging them in no-code creativity. Rather than simply a piece created with coding, it uses code to build a new participatory medium. Such a rationalization of technology and space shows the potential of expanding digital work beyond conventional displays and serves as an inspiration to follow suit.
